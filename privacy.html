What we collect

  • Enquiries. Your name, email address, business or profile name, the service you are interested in, and the message you send through the contact form or by email.
  • Purchases. The service bought, the amount, the date, your email address, and billing details you give to our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Service delivery. Information you choose to share so we can carry out the service you booked.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

Please do not send us passwords, recovery codes, authentication codes, or identity documents. We do not ask for them and do not need them.

How we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and decide whether we can help: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and steps taken at your request before a contract.
  • To take payment and deliver the service you bought: performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep accounting and tax records: legal obligation.
  • To protect the website from abuse and spam: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Use of AI tools

We use AI software to help sort enquiries, prepare internal notes, and draft replies. This means the content of your enquiry is processed by our AI provider on our behalf. A person remains responsible for the advice we give and for decisions about your service. We do not make decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you by automated means alone.

Who we share it with

We use these providers to run the business. They process data on our instructions:

  • Netlify: website hosting, server logs, and storage of enquiry records.
  • Stripe: payment processing. Stripe is an independent controller for the payment data it collects.
  • Anthropic: AI processing of enquiry content, as described above.
  • Forward Email and Google: receiving and sending email.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a booking: up to 12 months.
  • Customer and payment records: 6 years after the end of the tax year, as required for tax purposes.
  • Material you share for service delivery: deleted within 90 days of the service finishing, unless you ask us to keep it longer.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It saves your colour theme choice and your dismissal of the notice banner in your browser's local storage. That information stays on your device. Stripe may set its own cookies on its checkout page.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict or stop using it, and ask for it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email contact@bucuriedigital.com. We reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
